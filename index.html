Get started

Download css/webplate.css (and js/webplate.js if you want the click effects, tabs, toasts and modals). Add class="wp" to your <body>, then copy any example below.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="css/webplate.css">
<script src="js/webplate.js" defer></script>

<body class="wp">
  <button class="wp-btn wp-btn--primary">Hello</button>
</body>

Theming

All colours, corners and motion come from the tokens at the top of webplate.css. Change the accent and every button, focus ring and badge follows. Dark mode follows the visitor's system, or force it with data-theme="dark" on <html>.

:root {
  --wp-accent: #2f6fdf;       /* your brand colour */
  --wp-accent-hover: #2560c8;
  --wp-r: 6px;                /* sharper corners */
}
